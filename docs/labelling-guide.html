Crossing Labelling Guide

How to draw boxes in Roboflow from now on. Four classes, two questions per person, and a box around everyone you can see.

The four classes

Type the names exactly as shown: lowercase, with an underscore. The colours match the boxes the app draws.

id 0person_leftOn foot, moving towards the left edge of the photo
id 1person_rightOn foot, moving towards the right edge of the photo
id 2wheeled_leftA person with a bike or scooter, moving left
id 3wheeled_rightA person with a bike or scooter, moving right

Ask two questions for every person

Question 1 Do they have a bike or scooter?
  • Yes, riding it, walking beside it, or carrying it: wheeled_*. Draw one box around the person and the vehicle.
  • No: person_*, a box around the person.
  • A bike with nobody at it gets no box.
Question 2 Which way across the photo are they going?
  • Judge it as the camera sees it, not from the person's own left or right.
  • Heading towards the photo's left edge: _left. Towards the right edge: _right.
  • Walking straight towards or away from the camera: pick the side they are drifting to. Use their feet and the way their body is angled.
  • Everyone gets left or right, including people standing still. There is no "other" class.

Drawing the box

Do

  • Tight edges. Touch the top of the head, the soles of the feet, and the furthest arm or bag.
  • One box per person, even in a crowd where boxes overlap.
  • Small, far-away people count. Zoom in. If you can tell it is a person, label it.
  • Hidden people: box only the visible part. Do not guess where hidden legs would be.
  • Wheeled boxes run from the top of the rider's head to the bottom of the lowest wheel, and from the front wheel to the back wheel.

Never

  • Leave a visible person without a box. The model learns "this is not a person" and starts missing people.
  • Use a bicycle, cyclist or person_other class. Only the four above exist.
  • Draw a person box inside a wheeled box, or a separate box for the bike.
  • Turn on flip augmentation in Roboflow. A mirrored person going left is labelled left but is really going right. Leave all augmentation off: training adds its own.
  • Upload photos anywhere public. Keep them in the private team project only.

In Roboflow

While labelling

  1. Work in the private team project, c389_project_final.
  2. Tag every upload with where and when it was shot, e.g. crossing_A_2026-11-05, or outside_data for images not taken by us.
  3. Check the layers list on each image. A tiny stray box is easier to find there than on the picture.
  4. Pre-labelled images arrive with guessed directions. Check every person's direction, not only the flagged ones.
  5. The train / valid / test split in Roboflow does not matter. We re-split every export 70/20/10 in code so near-identical photos never land in different splits.

After each export (YOLO26 format)

Send the zip to whoever runs the scripts. They run, from the repo:

python -m src.data.remap_classes EXPORT --out FIXED python -m src.data.resplit FIXED --out SPLIT --ratio 70/20/10 python -m src.data.verify_export SPLIT --expect 4 python -m src.data.dedupe_check SPLIT

An export that still has a bicycle class goes through src.data.to_wheeled instead of remap_classes.

Team rule, agreed 3 Nov 2026: walking or carrying a bike counts as wheeled. Classes follow the lecturer's Twist 2 table. Questions about an odd case: post a screenshot in the group chat before guessing, and add the decision to this guide.
